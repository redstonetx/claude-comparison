§ 01

Two Generations

Claude 1.0
The
Foundation
March 2023

Anthropic's first publicly available model, built on Constitutional AI principles. Claude 1.0 established the baseline for helpful, harmless, and honest AI interaction— demonstrating that safety and capability could coexist. It featured a 9k token context window and strong performance on conversational tasks, setting the stage for rapid iteration.

vs
Claude 2.0
The
Leap
July 2023

A significant generational improvement across every measured dimension. Claude 2.0 expanded the context window to 100,000 tokens—enough to process entire books—while dramatically improving coding, mathematics, and long-form reasoning. Reduced harmful outputs and improved safety alignment marked a new standard for responsible AI deployment.

§ 05

Development Timeline

February 2023
Claude 1.0 Launch

Anthropic makes Claude available to the public through its API and claude.ai. The model demonstrates strong conversational ability and a distinctive character grounded in Constitutional AI training.

March — May 2023
Claude 1.x Iterations

Rapid point releases refine Claude 1's behavior, improve instruction-following, and reduce false refusals. Anthropic gathers extensive red-teaming data to inform the next major release.

July 11, 2023
Claude 2.0 Announced

A landmark release. Anthropic ships Claude 2 with a 100K token context window, substantially improved coding and mathematical reasoning, and measurably lower harmful output rates — available on both claude.ai and the API.

November 2023
Claude 2.1 Released

An incremental improvement extending context to 200K tokens, adding tool use capabilities, and reducing hallucination rates — further cementing Claude 2 as the gold standard for long-context reasoning.

Claude 1.0 · Legacy

The model that proved AI could be principled

Claude 1.0 was significant not just as a capable model, but as a demonstration that Constitutional AI could work at scale. For most conversational tasks, summarization, and moderate-length document processing, it performed admirably and laid critical groundwork for every Claude model that followed.

Conversational 9K Context Constitutional AI Pioneer Foundational
Claude 2.0 · Current

The leap that redefined what long-context AI can do

Claude 2.0 is not a refinement — it is a generational jump. The 100K context window alone unlocks entirely new use cases: full codebase analysis, book-length summarization, and extended reasoning chains. Combined with benchmark improvements across coding, math, and law, Claude 2.0 set the new frontier for safe and capable AI.
